Mapping which kinases we can actually model

A reproducible pipeline measuring how much experimental structure exists for every kinase domain in the human kinome. Of 499 domains, 160 are well covered, 149 thinly covered and 190 structurally dark, and 26 have no solved relative close enough to model from.

Python, UniProt/SIFTS, KLIFS, RCSB PDB, IDG

Ph.D. · 2021-2026

Finding inhibitors for a kinase with no complete structure

BUB1B is a target in metastatic prostate cancer with no complete experimental kinase domain and no validated inhibitor, so ordinary structure-based screening was not an option. I built a chimeric model that addresses where current deep learning structure predictors fall short on dark kinases. A multitask neural network then enriched multi-million-compound libraries, and ensemble docking with molecular dynamics prioritized candidates for biochemical testing.

Python, RDKit, AlphaFold3, IntFOLD, GROMACS, Maestro

Can a vision model judge a docked pose?

Testing whether a vision-language model picks the correct ligand pose better than GNINA CNN scoring when the pocket is slightly wrong, which is the situation whenever you screen against a predicted structure. 25 crystal structures, pocket error swept 0.5 to 3.0 Å, with a control that separates real robustness from a model quietly ignoring the protein.

Python, RDKit, GNINA, smina, PyMOL, ProLIF, PoseBusters
